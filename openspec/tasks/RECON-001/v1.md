# RECON-001 v1 — the two state editors (Car states, Customer states)

**Registry entry:** TASK-001 — UI (Layer 1), in `openspec/tasks-registry.md`. This file is the remaining piece of RECON-001: the two state-editor screens the UI layer is still missing.

**Form (owner ruling 2026-10-08):** this is a plan, not an instruction. It states what must be true and the process to follow. Completion is judged against the benchmarks, not against whether a verb was obeyed.

**Layer (owner ruling 2026-10-09):** UI only. This is Layer 1. Nothing here stores or saves anything — the screens render, like every other office screen in this layer.

---

## Why

The UI layer has no way to add or change a state. Every state is fixed in the build: the car form shows a hardcoded list (`Available`, `On the way`, `At port`, `Cleared`, `Landed`) and there is no screen anywhere to add, rename, reorder, colour, or delete one.

Two things require an editable state registry:

- **`specs/inventory`** — car states SHALL be an editable registry: add, rename, reorder; each state maps to exactly one section; each carries an owner-chosen colour and an optional new-arrival flag; deletion is blocked while any car sits in it.
- **`specs/enquiries`** — enquiry states SHALL be an owner-editable registry *like car states*: add, rename, reorder; deletion blocked while any enquiry sits in the state.

And **`ui-plan.md` Q58** puts both on the office Settings page as **Car states** and **Customer states**. So two screens are missing, and the report's "state editor — done" row is wrong.

---

## Scope

**In scope — two office screens, UI only:**

- **Car states** — the editor table from `ui-plan.md` Q59.
- **Customer states** — the same editor for the enquiry states, minus the two car-only controls.
- The Settings page's **left sub-menu** (General / Car states / Customer states / Account), which does not exist today.

**Out of scope:** everything else. No storing, saving, mapping rules, or delete-guard logic — those are other layers. The screens render from the built-in state list, as the rest of this layer does.

---

## What must be true

1. **The Settings page has a left sub-menu** — General / Car states / Customer states / Account (Q58) — and Car states and Customer states open their editors.

2. **The Car states editor** is a table with, per row:
   - a **drag handle** — drag a row up or down to set the order the states appear in;
   - **name** (editable);
   - a **group dropdown** — the section the state shows under, one of Available / On Order / Landed, and one is always set;
   - a **colour picker** with a **live sample of the label** beside it, so the state's colour and its readable ink are seen together;
   - a **"Show as a tab" switch** — an inventory filter only, never a showroom tab;
   - the **number of cars using it**;
   - a **delete button**, disabled with a tooltip while any car is in that state.
   - and an **add** action for a new state.

3. **The Customer states editor** is the same table — drag handle, name, **colour picker** with live label sample, number of enquiries using it, delete disabled with a tooltip while any enquiry is in that state, and an add action. It has **no group dropdown** and **no "Show as a tab" switch** — those are car-only.

4. **The colour picker behaves the same in both editors:** one colour per state, chosen from the picker (no random generator), the written label always present beside the colour, and the label ink worked out by contrast so any chosen colour stays readable.

5. **Both screens hold at all three widths (phone / tablet / laptop) and in both modes (dark default, light toggle).**

6. **Nothing beyond these two screens is built here** — no persistence, no mapping enforcement, no delete-guard rules.

## Decisions recorded — Round 1 (structure and behaviour), 2026-10-12

1. **The sub-menu switches in place.** One route (`/office/settings`); the four items swap which content is shown. No new routes — Q58 says "one page with a left sub-menu", and nothing needs shareable URLs.

2. **General keeps three cards** — WhatsApp number, Tagline, Currency & cost. **Account holds the Password card** (a password is account material, and Q58's General list does not include it, so it does not belong there). Nothing else is invented for Account. *This changes the round-1 proposal, which left Password in General and made Account a placeholder — flagged for the owner.*

3. **Hours and social links are not built** (Q58 names them; they belong to a later element). General is otherwise unchanged.

4. **At phone width** the sub-menu becomes a horizontal, scrollable tab row under the page title — the same four items, the same order, the active one highlighted.

5. **Edits are live and in memory.** No save button; everything resets on reload. A save button on an editor that cannot persist would be a lie this layer can't honour. Add appends a new row named "New state", inline-editable.

6. **Drag** = native HTML5 drag, the pattern the enquiries board already uses, **plus** a keyboard-accessible way to move a row (focusable, no visible clutter).

7. **Label ink** = chosen by relative luminance — whichever of near-black or white reads against the picked colour — computed, and shown on the live sample beside the picker.

8. **Seed colours — reuse only what already exists, invent nothing:**
   - **Car states:** Available = the office green (`--ok`); On the way and At port = blue `#3a7bd5`; Landed = gold (`--warn`); Cleared = its section's colour (gold). **Special is not an editable row** — it is the ribbon, fixed gold.
   - **Customer states:** New `#3a7bd5`, Talking `#b9820b`, Negotiating `#c8a24a`, Sold `#0e7a3e`, Lost `#8a9096` — the board's dot colours.
   - **Divergence logged, not silently settled:** `ui-plan.md`'s State-labels table gives the section colours as Available green / On Order gold / Landed gold, which would make On the way and At port gold rather than blue. The reference — and the office as built — shows them blue. The editor seeds from what exists; the owner's call whether the section colours should change.

9. **Counts** = the demo rows the office already renders (`lib/office.ts`) that sit in that state — zero for an empty state. Delete is disabled while the count is above zero, with a tooltip saying why.

### Decisions recorded — Round 2 (the branches), 2026-10-12

10. **Section colours stay as built; the divergence is logged, not settled here.** The editor seeds On the way and At port blue, matching the reference and the office as built. ui-plan's State-labels table (On Order = gold) is **not** applied in this task — changing it would ripple into the inventory chips, the car-form pills and the showroom labels, all outside this task. The conflict stays logged for a later ruling. The editor seeds from the palette; it does not change it.

11. **"Show as a tab" starts ON for all five seeded car states** — they are the inventory filters the office already shows. (The office as built chips Available / On the way / At port / Landed / Sold; "Cleared" is one of the five seeded states but is not currently chipped — that is the inventory screen's business, not the editor's.) Customer states have no switch.

12. **A newly added row:** group = the first section (Available) — a section is always set, and the dropdown corrects the moment it is touched; colour = neutral until picked (label in normal ink, no chip); "Show as a tab" = ON, matching the seeds. A new customer state = neutral colour until picked.

13. **Settings opens on General** — the page as it is today (three cards), with Car states / Customer states / Account one click away. The URL stays `/office/settings` whichever panel is showing.
