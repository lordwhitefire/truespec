How to read it

You do not need to read it all at once. Start with 01 and 02. Then do 03 — it takes five minutes and makes the site yours. The rest is a reference: read the page you need, when you need it.

Words in bold are the exact words you will see on the screen. If this guide says Save car, there is a button called Save car.

The one thing to remember

Your cost and your profit never leave the office. Not on the page, not in the data the page receives, not in the browser's developer tools. The server enforces it — it is not a setting and not something you can turn off by accident.

01

Read this first

You have two websites in one. They look different because they are for different people.

The showroom

This is what a customer sees. Anyone can open it, from anywhere, without a password. They can look at cars, see the one price you have set, and start a WhatsApp conversation with you. They cannot change anything. They cannot see what a car cost you.

The office

This is what you see. It is private. It holds your stock, your costs, your profit, your enquiries, and the record of every change. Nobody reaches it without signing in.

Showroom addresshttps://truespec-k4y6.vercel.app
Office addresshttps://truespec-k4y6.vercel.app/office
Who can get inYou, and anyone you invite. There is no public sign-up.

One rule that governs everything

A customer can see the doorstep price — the one number he pays. He can never see the purchase price, the shipping, the clearing, the trucking, the full tank, the landed cost, or the profit. Those live in the office only.

The cars you will see are sample cars

They are there so the site does not look empty on day one, and so you can practise. Replace them with your own, or delete them. Nothing you do to a sample car matters.

What you can safely do

Everything in this guide is safe to try. Nothing is permanent except a saved car, a saved enquiry, and the activity log — and even a deleted car can be brought back with Undo.

Your first five minutes

Do these five things now. After them, the site is yours — not a demonstration.

1

Put in your real WhatsApp number

Open Settings → WhatsApp number. Type your number with the country code, starting with +234. Select Save number.

The site ships with a placeholder number. Until you change it, every chat button on the site opens a chat with the wrong number. This is the single most important thing on this page.

2

Write your tagline

Settings → Tagline. One short line under the brand on the home page. Type it, watch the preview, select Save tagline.

3

Set your full tank default

Settings → Full tank. Type the amount you usually spend filling a tank. Select Save default. Every new car will start with this figure; you can change it on any car.

4

Add one real car

Select Add (or Add / edit car on a computer). Fill in what you know. You do not have to finish every box — a car saves with what it has. Select Save car.

5

Go and look at it in the showroom

Open https://truespec-k4y6.vercel.app in another tab. Your car is there, with its doorstep price. Everything you kept in the office stayed in the office.

04

The showroom — what customers see

Four pages. No login. Nothing here can be changed by a visitor.

Home

The front door. It has a large opening image, the brand, your tagline, and a Chat on WhatsApp button that is never far away.

Below that, three short lines highlight something from your stock — a new arrival, a car about to land, and a price that has dropped. They come from the cars you have entered; you do not write them by hand.

Then a handful of featured cars, and a small panel that asks What are you after? A visitor can tap A family SUV, A saloon, or Something I haven't listed yet. It opens a WhatsApp message already written for him. He can also select Skip.

Inventory

Every car, in one place. At the top there are filters: Status, Make, Year, Colour, Price. The status filter holds the three sections — Available, On Order, Landed this year — with the number of cars in each.

There is a sort control (Newest first, Price: low to high, Price: high to low, Year: newest) and a search. Each car shows its photo, name, price, mileage and colour. Selecting it opens the car page.

Car page

One car in full: a photo gallery, the car's facts, and three tabs — Overview, Features, Delivery.

The price shown is the doorstep price: shipping, clearing and delivery already inside it. There is no second number anywhere on this page.

The visitor can Chat on WhatsApp, Share this, and — depending on the car's state — Book a viewing or Reserve this car. If he has looked at this car before, the page greets him by name of the car and offers to book.

Privacy notice

A plain page telling visitors what is stored. The short version: no accounts, no forms, no analytics; the only thing kept is the record that a WhatsApp button was tapped, and on which car.

Why this matters to you

Every WhatsApp tap is recorded in your office. When someone taps a button on the BMW, you will see “Tap on BMW X5” — not his name, because the site never asks for it. The name arrives when he messages you.

06

Dashboard

Three numbers and three counts. This is the page you open first in the morning.

The money

What it saysWhat it means
Money tied upWhat you have spent on stock you still hold. It includes cars whose costs are not fully entered, because that money is already spent.
Projected profitWhat you would make if every car sold at its current price. Cars with incomplete costs are left out, and the page tells you how many.
Real profit (sold)What you have actually made on cars marked sold — sale price minus landed cost.

If Projected profit says some cars are excluded, select See which. It takes you to the cars that need their costs finished. A car with a missing cost is not wrong — it is just not counted until it is complete.

The counts

Available, On order, and Landed — the same three states customers browse. New arrival is counted inside Available and is also shown as a small figure of its own.

The two lists

Recent activity is the last few things that happened, with who did them. Recent WhatsApp taps shows which cars people tapped. Full log and All taps open the longer versions.

If a number looks wrong

Almost always, one car has an incomplete cost, or a car was marked sold with no sale price. Open Inventory, filter, and look. The activity log will tell you who changed what.

Inventory

Every car you hold, what it cost, and what it should make. This page is never public.

The columns

ColumnWhat it shows
CarName, reference number (TS-####) and mileage. Select it to edit.
StateAvailable, Landed, On Order, or Sold.
PriceThe doorstep price a customer pays.
CostEverything you spent to land the car.
ProfitPrice minus cost. Red or blank means something is incomplete.
ActionsEdit, and a More menu with keep, delete and undo.

The tabs

All, Available, Landed, On Order, Sold. Each carries its count. All includes sold cars; the others do not.

Finding a car

  • Use Newest first, Price: high to low, Price: low to high or Profit: high to low to sort.
  • Choose how many cars to a page: 10, 25, 50 or 100.
  • Costs incomplete appears beside a cost that is missing a figure. Select it to open that car and finish it.

Two ways to create

Add a car opens a blank form — the normal way, one car at a time.

Create from file takes a filled PDF. Its rows do not become cars. They become drafts, waiting for you in Drafts to check before saving. There is also a Car template you can download, fill in, and send back.

Why drafts exist

A file full of cars is easy to get wrong, and hard to check once it is live. A draft lets you see each car before the world does. Nothing reaches the showroom until you select Save car.

08

Add a car — the car and the photos

One form, five numbered sections. You can stop at any point and save.

Open it with Add on a phone, or Add / edit car on a computer.

1 — The car

These are the facts a buyer reads.

Brand, Model, Trim, YearWrite them as the car reads — Toyota, Camry, XSE V6, 2021.
MileageIn kilometres, exactly as shown on the car.
Doorstep price (₦)The one price a buyer sees. Shipping, clearing and delivery are already inside it.
Was price (₦)Optional. If you use it, it appears crossed out beside the price. Leave it blank for no “was” price.
Exterior colour, InteriorPlain words — Obsidian black, Black.
Features — one per lineEach line becomes a bullet on the car's page. Press Enter after each one.
Note (buyers read this)Shown on the car's page. Anything a buyer should know — an arrival date, a small flaw, a reason to call.

2 — Photos

Drag photos onto the box, or select it and choose them from your phone or computer. You can add several at once.

  • The first photo is the cover — the one buyers see in the list. Choose it deliberately.
  • Each photo can be up to 15 MB.
  • You can reorder and remove them before saving.

About photo size

The site keeps photos exactly as you upload them. It does not shrink them yet. Normal phone photos are fine; a very large photo will make the page slower for buyers. If a page feels slow, start by checking the photos on it.

Shortcut: Autofill from PDF

If you already have the car's details in a PDF, select Autofill from PDF and choose the file. The form fills itself where it can. Check every box afterwards — a machine read the file, not you.

Add a car — costs, state, private notes

3 — Costs — never leaves the office

Six boxes. Type what you spent, in naira:

Purchase priceShippingClearingTrucking within USATrucking in NigeriaFull tank

Full tank comes pre-filled from your Settings default. Change it on any car where the figure was different.

As you type, a panel on the side keeps a running Landed cost. When you have entered a doorstep price as well, it shows Profit on this car. You do not calculate anything.

What the buyer sees

Beside the form is a small panel called What the buyer sees. It shows exactly what will be public: the facts, the photos, and the doorstep price. Your costs, your profit and your notes are not in it — because they are never sent to the buyer's browser at all.

4 — State

Choose where the car is. The showroom follows automatically.

StateWhere it appears
AvailableIn the showroom, for sale now.
On OrderIn the showroom, as a car on the way. Buyers can reserve it.
LandedIn the showroom, under Landed this year.
SoldOut of the public showroom, kept in your records with its real profit.

Tracking states are optional and office-only: On the way, At port, Cleared. Use them if a car passes through several stages. Buyers never see them and they do not change any count.

New arrival is a small flag. It shows the car under “Landed this year” in the showroom as well as Available.

Arrival note is shown to buyers on On Order cars — the one thing they would otherwise have to ask about. Write “Expected arrival: November”, for example.

5 — Private notes

Notes to yourself. Who you bought from, what to remember, what not to say. Office-only, forever.

Save

Select Save car. The car appears in the showroom immediately — there is no separate publish step. If you are not ready, select Cancel, or leave and come back.

Before saving, you can select Preview the buyer page to see it exactly as a customer would.

10

Change a car, sell it, delete it

Change anything

Go to Inventory and select Edit beside the car — or select the car's name. The same five-section form opens, filled in. Change what you need, then select Save car. The showroom updates immediately.

Mark a car as sold

Open the car and select Mark as sold. The state changes to Sold and two fields appear: Sale price (₦) and the sale date. Fill both, then Save car.

The car leaves the public showroom. In the office it stays — with the real profit you made, not the projected one. That is the figure on the Dashboard under Real profit (sold).

Delete a car

Open the car and select Delete. Confirm. The car leaves the showroom and the inventory list.

If you deleted it by mistake, select Undo — it is offered straight away, and the car comes back. Every delete and restore is recorded in the Activity log, so you can always see what happened and when.

What “Sold” does to your numbers

A sold car stops counting as stock and stops counting in Projected profit. It starts counting in Real profit. If a sale is recorded without a sale price, the profit cannot be worked out — so the Dashboard will tell you something is missing.

The reference number

Every car gets a TS-#### number. It is how the office, the activity log and the WhatsApp taps refer to that car. You do not choose it and you cannot change it. Use it when you need to talk about a specific car with someone.

11

Drafts — files you upload

A draft is a car or an enquiry that has been read from a file but is not yet a record. Nothing in Drafts is public.

How a file becomes a draft

  1. On Inventory, select Create from file and choose a filled car template.
  2. Or on Enquiries, select Create from file and choose the sales desk's sheet.
  3. The site reads the file and puts what it found into Drafts.
  4. Open Drafts, check each one, correct anything, and save it.

Only PDF files are accepted. The site decides what kind of draft to make by reading the file, not by which page you uploaded it from.

What you see in Drafts

Files are grouped in batches, newest first. Under each file are the rows it produced — some car drafts, some enquiry drafts.

Each draft shows what was read and where it came from. Select Edit to open it. A car draft opens the same five-section car form; an enquiry draft opens name, phone number, what they asked for, note, and state.

What you can do with a draft

ActionWhat happens
EditCorrect anything the file got wrong, or fill what it could not carry.
Save as a carThe draft becomes a real car and appears in the showroom immediately.
Save as a enquiryThe draft becomes a real enquiry on your board. No car reference number is used up.
DeleteMoves it aside. Remove for good deletes it permanently.

A refused row is named

If the file has a row the site cannot read, it does not disappear quietly. The upload tells you which row or page was refused and why. Fix it in the file and upload again, or enter that car by hand.

Photos in a draft

A PDF cannot carry the photos. On an enquiry draft you can add the pictures the file could not bring. For a car draft, add the photos in the car form before you save it.

12

Enquiries

Two views of the same thing: the people who talked to you, and the taps that started it.

Enquiries and Taps

Enquiries is your board of customers. Taps is the raw record of someone pressing a WhatsApp button on the site.

A tap tells you only three things: that it happened, which car, and when. It carries no name and no number — the site never asks for them. The name arrives when the person actually messages you.

The board

Enquiries move through five columns: New, Talking, Negotiating, Sold, Lost. Move a card as the conversation moves.

Each card shows the person's initials, name, date, phone number, an ENQ-#### reference, what they asked for, and your note.

What you can do with an enquiry

  • WhatsApp them — opens a chat with that number.
  • Call — opens the dialler.
  • Edit — change the name, number, what they asked for, the note, or the state.
  • Delete enquiry — remove it from the board.

Where enquiries come from

From a file. Select Create from file and upload the filled Enquiry template or the sales desk's sheet. Its rows arrive in Drafts, where you check them and select Save enquiry.

There is no blank “add enquiry” form. An enquiry always starts as a row in a file, so the board matches whatever record the conversation came from.

The weekly rhythm

Check Taps in the morning — that is who reached out. Bring the names back from your WhatsApp and the sales sheet. Move the cards as you talk. The board is only useful if the state is true.

13

Activity log

Every change made to a car, who made it, and when. Entries cannot be edited or removed — that is what makes the log worth trusting.

What is recorded

Adding a car, editing it, changing its state, marking it sold, deleting it, restoring it, importing a file, and changing settings. Each entry shows the car's reference, the action, the time, and the account that did it.

The filters

AllAddedEditedStateSoldDeletedRestoredImportsSettings

Each carries its count. Select one to narrow the list.

When to use it

When a price looks different from how you remember it. When a car has vanished. When two people use the office and you need to know who did what. The log answers “what happened here?” without anyone having to remember.

15

What changed from the brief, and why

Some requirements were met exactly. A few were met a different way, because the different way was better. Here they are, plainly.

Where I chose a different way

  • The back office is called “Office”, not “Admin”. It is your back room, and the word is used everywhere — the menu, the sign-in screen, the address. The separation the brief asked for is unchanged.
  • One Inventory page instead of three separate sections. Available, On Order and Landed are now filters on one catalogue. A buyer comparing cars does not have to remember which section a car was in, and when a car moves from On Order to Available it does not disappear from where he was looking.
  • Enquiries were added. The brief asked for a WhatsApp button. A button tells you nothing. The site now records that a tap happened and on which car, and gives you a board for the names and numbers that follow. This is the largest addition, and it is the part you will use most.
  • Drafts were added. A filled PDF is checked before it becomes live stock. A file read straight into the showroom is an accident waiting to happen.
  • Selling was added. The brief asked for projected profit. The site also records the sale price and shows what you actually made.
  • Your enquiry states are yours. Add, rename, colour and reorder them to match the way you actually talk about a sale.

The one thing not delivered

The brief asked for photos to be automatically compressed and resized when uploaded. That is not built yet. Photos are kept exactly as you upload them. In practice: use normal phone photos, do not upload several very large ones to one car, and check the page if it feels slow.

What was added on the public site

The one-tap What are you after? panel, the three short lines on the home page, the share button, and the greeting for a visitor who returns to a car. None of them change what data is public. All of them are there to start a conversation.
