Send as the email body, with `MVP-User-Guide.pdf` attached. Keep it this short. The guide does the
explaining; the note does the accounting.

---

**Subject:** TrueSpec — the honest version, and the guide

So this isn't a demo. It's your site, working, and it's yours to run.

The attached guide is plain English and page by page. I've sent your invitation to this address
separately — open it and sign in with Google. Then start at page 3: the five minutes that makes the

[Your name]

## Notes on the draft

- **The apology is one sentence and it names the silence, not the delay.** "I didn't tell you when I
  realised it had changed" is the sentence that does the work. Do not cut it, and do not add a second
  apology later — a repeat reads as anxiety and makes him manage you.
- **No blame anywhere.** The brief is never held responsible for the delay. It is only ever described
  as what it is (a production brief), never as the cause of anything.
- **"At five weeks" is stated once.** Not hidden, not repeated. If he asks, the answer is the same
  sentence.
- **The last line before the guide is the value, not the apology.** The note ends on "yours to run",
  not on "sorry".
- **Placeholders to fill:** `[Name]`, `[Your name]`. Nothing else.
