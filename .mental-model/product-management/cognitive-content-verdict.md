## Item-by-item

### 1. CS-1 / D-0 / J1 — COGNITION
The model resolves a live alternative (run all seven steps vs. judge coverage against the question) and lands on a discriminator a reader would have to think to reach: completeness is relative to the question, coverage stays non-negotiable while visibility is negotiable ("checklist, not a recipe", "hammer when a scalpel would have sufficed") — that is a threshold and a priority, not a description of the framework.

### 2. CS-2 / D-2 — COGNITION
"Bluff or admit" is posed as a decision with a resolution and an ordered remedy (admit, then ask the 3W's and H, then get a visual in front of the interviewer because a mismatch in mental models would be lethal) — a choice plus a rule for acting under a named trigger, not a fact about unfamiliar products.

### 3. CS-3 / D-3 — COGNITION
Stall-vs-assume is resolved to assume-and-hand-back, and the basis carries the actual safety reasoning (the assumption is provisional and the interviewer holds standing to overturn it, which is what separates a provisional assumption from a fabrication) — that reasoning had to be produced, not read off the page.

### 4. CS-4 / D-4 — COGNITION
One persona or several is decided (one, with the reason stated) and justified on a trade-off the model states itself (an all-in-one that serves everyone is usually mediocre; the interviewer may not know the persona, so it must be made legible on the 2x2) — a choice, a rationale and a presentation rule.

### 5. CS-5 / D-5 — COGNITION
Job-or-user-story is decided (user story), with the operative constraint that the story must exclude the solution and the payoff it buys (one sentence carries user, need and justification), plus object discipline keeping Lin's Report-on-a-need distinct from Torres's test-on-an-opportunity — that distinction is precedence reasoning.

### 6. CS-6 / D-6 — COGNITION
Under two use cases each demanding different solutions and time for one, the model decides to cut on a matrix that includes ease of implementation, and reasons why effort belongs in this cut (a user story is already scoped work, so its effort is knowable) while explicitly reconciling that with D-13 where effort is excluded — that reconciliation is precedence reasoning about objects, not a fact about prioritization.

### 7. D-13 — COGNITION
The instruction to name the object before applying any rule, with effort excluded on an opportunity (an opportunity assumed too hard may have a dead-simple solution) and included on a story, is a discrimination rule a reader could not produce without thinking — it prevents a rule from being carried to the wrong object.

### 8. D-30 — COGNITION
Root-cause-first via Five Whys is chosen over reaching for the solution, and the model shows its working on the worked chain (the fourth why becomes a solution, the fifth becomes a constraint), flags that the depth is its own reading rather than a stated stopping rule, and distinguishes a root-cause instrument from Torres's reframing test — a judgment including an admission about the limit of the inference.

### 9. CS-25 / J23 — COGNITION
The cue encodes a diagnostic method rather than a fact: decompose the moved metric into a structured tree (geography, device, browser, source, down the funnel) and walk every branch instead of settling on one, while carrying the chosen top metric's own known drawbacks as a separately tracked mitigation; the paired criterion demands naming which metric leads with business-model reasoning and defending a choice rather than enumerating a list — both are rules for acting under diagnostic and selection uncertainty.

### 10. CS-29 / D-37 / J31 — COGNITION
This is an inversion of an intuitive compliment: safe and small is a defect signal, resolved as reach past the obvious fix and bring one audacious option, with originality treated as a precondition that requires actually surveying the existing space; the model also draws the discriminating line that the corpus means ambition, not risk-taking — a priority and a threshold.

### 11. CS-31 / D-39 / J33 — COGNITION
Ordering is the content: agree the frame before reasoning inside it, fix what a criterion means before scoring against it, lead with the instrument, answer every part asked — and the model separates this from the earlier persona decision as a different failure, showing the priority is about the meaning of terms rather than about who the product is for.

## Summary
All eleven items are COGNITION. None is a definition, a piece of domain trivia, a description of what something is, or a bare quotation: each carries an alternative weighed and resolved, a threshold, a priority, an ordering rule, or an object-discrimination that had to be reasoned to. Several (6, 7, 8, 11) additionally perform precedence reasoning across neighbouring entries.
